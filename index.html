    Reading · Plastic Pollution

    Plastic Pollution

    Read the text. Then find the English words and answer the questions. Get every answer right to win one star.

    Plastic is everywhere. People use it for bottles, bags, cups and toys. It is light and cheap, but it is also a big problem. Plastic does not disappear. A plastic bottle can stay on a beach or in the sea for hundreds of years.

    Animals sometimes think plastic is food. A turtle can eat a plastic bag because it looks like a jellyfish. Seabirds give small pieces of plastic to their babies. The animals become ill.

    Plastic also breaks into tiny pieces. These pieces are called microplastics. They are in the sea, in rivers, and even in some of the food we eat.

    We can help. Carry a reusable bottle. Take a cloth bag to the shop. Say no to a plastic straw. Recycle bottles and put them in the right bin. Small actions protect the ocean and the animals that live there.

    Find the word

    Read the French word. Find that word in the text and write it in English.

    1. une bouteille
    2. un sac
    3. une tortue
    4. la nourriture
    5. une rivière
    6. une paille

    Questions

    Choose one answer for each question.

    1. How long can a plastic bottle stay in the sea?

    2. Why can a turtle eat a plastic bag?

    3. What are the tiny pieces of plastic called?

    4. Which action helps the ocean?

    Reading · Harry Potter

    Harry Potter

    Read the text. Then find the English words and answer the questions. Get every answer right to win one star.

    Harry Potter is the hero of a famous story. He is a boy with black hair and green eyes. He lives with his aunt, his uncle and his cousin. They are not kind. Harry sleeps in a tiny room under the stairs.

    On his eleventh birthday, everything changes. Harry learns that he is a wizard. His parents were wizards too, but they died when Harry was a baby. A giant called Hagrid comes to the house. He takes Harry away from his uncle’s family.

    Harry goes to a school for witches and wizards. The school is called Hogwarts. There he meets two friends, Ron and Hermione. They study magic, they fly on broomsticks, and they play a game called Quidditch. Harry is the youngest player in the team.

    Harry has a scar on his forehead. It looks like a lightning bolt. A dangerous wizard gave him this scar. At Hogwarts, Harry and his friends are brave. They look after each other when there is trouble.

    Find the word

    Read the French word. Find that word in the text and write it in English.

    1. un anniversaire
    2. un sorcier
    3. un géant
    4. une école
    5. un balai
    6. une cicatrice

    Questions

    Choose one answer for each question.

    1. Where does Harry sleep at his aunt and uncle’s house?

    2. How old is Harry when he learns he is a wizard?

    3. What is the name of Harry’s school?

    4. What does Harry’s scar look like?

    Writing · Introduce yourself

    Introduce yourself

    Read Amira’s introduction. Then write your own. Use full sentences.

    Example

    Hello! My name is Amira. I am 11 years old. I live in Bristol with my mum, my dad and my brother. I have one brother. His name is Sam and he is eight. I do not have a sister. In my free time I like drawing and playing football. My favourite colour is green. My favourite food is pasta.

    Try to write about

    • your name
    • your age
    • where you live
    • your brothers and sisters
    • your hobbies
    • your favourite colour
    • your favourite food

    Your writing stays on this computer. When you have finished, mark this as done to win one star.
